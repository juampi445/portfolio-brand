# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Small-business owners and founders (shops, local businesses, early companies) evaluating whether to hire Juan Pablo Cuadrelli to build their website. They arrive from a referral or a search, skim on desktop or phone, and decide within a minute whether this person is credible. Bilingual audience: English and Spanish (Tandil, Argentina base).

## Product Purpose

A one-person-studio portfolio. The site is the proof: businesses that "need to look established from the first screen" should see that standard demonstrated by the portfolio itself. Success is a message through the contact form or a direct email.

## Brand Personality

Assured, direct, understated. Copy is first-person and plain ("You talk to the developer building your site, not an account manager"). The visual language backs that up: near-black ink (#111) and paper (#ECECEC), a single mint accent (#47D7B5 on dark, #286456 on light), hairline rules instead of boxes, restrained motion (small ease-out-quint settles, no theatrics).

## Anti-references

- Template-shaped freelancer portfolios: skill-bar percentages, identical service card grids, testimonial carousels.
- Agency bombast: parallax everything, oversized cursor effects, buzzword copy.
- Anything that reads as a widget dropped onto the page instead of part of it.

## Design Principles

- **The site is the pitch.** Every surface must itself look "established from the first screen"; craft is the argument, not a claim in the copy.
- **Lines, not boxes.** Hierarchy through hairline rules, scale, and spacing; containers only when they earn their place.
- **One accent, spent deliberately.** Mint appears where attention or action lives (links, focus, the CTA), nowhere else.
- **Motion means arrival.** Elements settle into place once; nothing loops, bounces, or re-fires on scroll.
- **Both languages are first-class.** Every string ships in English and Spanish; copy quality is equal in both.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Reduced motion is respected everywhere (animations collapse to static states). Visible labels on all form fields, errors named in text rather than colour alone, focus-visible outlines in the accent. Keep contrast workable on both the ink and paper surfaces.
